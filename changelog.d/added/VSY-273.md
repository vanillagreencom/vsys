- `vsys --lane SCOPE` opens Agents on the lane running in that systemd scope, and `vsys --lane SCOPE --stop` opens that lane's Stop confirmation. The Stop still runs only with write mode on and after you confirm it. A scope with no lane, or a lane that offers no Stop, shows a warning naming it. When vsys starts before its process read answers, it opens nothing and shows a warning that the process read has not answered.
