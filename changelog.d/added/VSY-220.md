- Home raises a card when a system service averages a quarter of a CPU core or more for an hour, such as a service stuck in a loop. The card names the service, its hour average and the command that restarts it, and the Timeline records one alert for each time this happens. Containers and mounts under the system slice are not reported. No service is judged until vsys has an hour of its readings, including after a gap in sampling or with a refresh interval over 15 minutes. Set the threshold with `serviceCpuPercent`.
