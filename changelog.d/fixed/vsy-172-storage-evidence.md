- Storage keeps confirmed damage and new errors when a scrub report cannot be read. An unreadable report with unknown order leaves filesystem health unknown at startup and after report replacement, including filesystems with reports for multiple mounts. A report proved older or unrelated cannot displace a newer readable check. Saved drive figures require matching current serial numbers, including SATA and SCSI drives. Restored boot ID access recovers checksum failures without assigning old failures to later-mounted filesystems.
